01 · Introduction

Introduction

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Wampo is a personal project by Shunsuke Maeda. This policy explains what the Wampo app collects, how it uses that information, and where it is sent.

02 · Information we collect

Information we collect

Here is what the app handles.

Location (GPS)

We use location to record walk routes and to show weather and rain radar.

  • Walk routes: stored on the device.
  • Weather and suggested walk times: we send the latitude and longitude of your current location, or a place you saved, to Open-Meteo. The Home Screen widget sends latitude and longitude for the same reason.
  • Rain radar: we send nearby map tile coordinates to the Japan Meteorological Agency.
  • Arrival notification: the app checks on the device whether you are near a home location you saved. That home location stays on the device.

Camera and photos

We use the camera and photo library for your dog's profile photo and for photos you attach to a walk. On a walk's detail screen, the app may look at timestamps of photos on the device to suggest shots from around that walk. Photos stay on the device. We do not upload them.

Notifications

Home-arrival alerts and reminders are local notifications. We do not send identifiers or location to a developer server to push them.

Apple Watch, Live Activity, and widgets

Walk progress and marker actions can appear on your Apple Watch, Lock Screen, and Home Screen widget. This stays between your own devices (Watch Connectivity and App Groups). Walk records are not sent to our servers this way.

Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Only if you agree, we send a device identifier and in-app actions (starting or finishing a walk, switching tabs, adding a marker, and similar) to Firebase Analytics (Google). We use this only to understand usage and improve the app. We do not send walk distance or duration, route coordinates, photos, or dog profiles. Nothing is sent until you agree. You can turn it off later in Settings.

03 · How we use location

How we use location

Location is used only for the features above. We do not use it for ads, marketing, or to build a list of who walked where.

If you choose Always Allow, the app can keep recording the route while the phone is in your pocket. You can still start a walk with While Using the App.

05 · Storage

Storage

Walk records (routes, distance, duration, markers, dog profiles, photos) stay in a database on the device. We do not upload them to a developer cloud. There is no sign-up.

06 · Sharing

Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not give it to anyone except as described above. What leaves the device is the minimum location needed for features, plus usage analytics after you agree.

07 · Deleting data and withdrawing consent

Deleting data and withdrawing consent

  • Records on the device: deleting the app deletes the walk records and profiles stored on the device.
  • Usage analytics: turn it off in Settings to withdraw consent. After that, we do not send new data to Firebase Analytics. How long Google keeps older data follows Google's policy.
  • Location, camera, photos, and notifications: you can turn off access for this app in iOS Settings at any time.

There is no in-app account deletion step, because there is no account.
