01 · Introduction

Introduction

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Wampo is a personal project by Shunsuke Maeda. This policy explains what the Wampo app collects, how it uses that information, and where it is sent.

02 · Information we collect

Information we collect

Here is what the app handles.

Location (GPS)

We use location to record walk routes and to show weather and rain radar.

  • Walk routes: stored on the device. For a walk with a dog you added to a group, the route and marker locations are sent to that group after the walk ends, unless Privacy Mode is on (see Account and groups). Your location is never sent live during a walk.
  • Weather and suggested walk times: we send the latitude and longitude of your current location, or a place you saved, to Open-Meteo. The Home Screen widget sends latitude and longitude for the same reason.
  • Rain radar: we send nearby map tile coordinates to the Japan Meteorological Agency.
  • Arrival notification: the app checks on the device whether you are near a home location you saved. That home location stays on the device and is not sent to groups.

Camera and photos

We use the camera and photo library for your dog's profile photo and for photos you attach to a walk. On a walk's detail screen, the app may look at timestamps of photos on the device to suggest shots from around that walk. Photos stay on the device. Only the photos and videos you choose for a group post are sent to that group. Capture location and other metadata are removed from the files we send. The capture location is sent to that group separately from the file, and only while Privacy Mode is off (see Account and groups).

Microphone and speech recognition

We use the microphone and the OS speech recognizer only when you start voice input for a walk-marker memo. Opening the app does not prompt for these permissions. Recognized text goes into the memo field. The app does not keep audio files or raw recognition logs. Speech recognition is an Apple or Google OS feature. We do not send audio to our own servers.

Notifications

Home-arrival alerts and reminders are local notifications. We do not send identifiers or location to a developer server for them.

If you are in a group, we send push notifications for new posts and replies, and Plus deadline notices to the group admin. For this, we store the registration of each device that allows notifications (a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token and an identifier for each app installation) and which notifications you want to receive on our server. Notifications contain fixed text only. They never include post text, names, photos, or location.

Apple Watch, Live Activity, and widgets

Walk progress and marker actions can appear on your Apple Watch, Lock Screen, and Home Screen widget. This stays between your own devices (Watch Connectivity and App Groups). Walk records are not sent to our servers this way.

Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Only if you agree, we send a device identifier and in-app actions (starting or finishing a walk, switching tabs, adding a marker, and similar) to Firebase Analytics (Google). We use this only to understand usage and improve the app. We do not send walk distance or duration, route coordinates, photos, dog profiles, or the identifier of your signed-in account. Nothing is sent until you agree. You can turn it off later in Settings.

Automatic walk comments retired

In 1.16.0 (213) and later, AI-generated and on-device template comments have been retired. The app no longer generates or displays automatic comments, sends data to Gemini, or offers the dedicated setting. Manually entered marker comments, notes, and walk records remain available.

Older versions and past transmissions

In feature-bearing versions 1.16.0 (212) and earlier, automatic comments and external transmissions may continue until you update. Returning to an older version may also resume transmissions under previously saved consent. The following describes the feature immediately before retirement; sent fields and consent scope vary with the older implementation. Retirement does not mean previously sent data has been deleted from Google’s systems.

Only for completed walks with at least 20 minutes of total elapsed time, and only with your separate consent, the app checks eligibility on the device and sends walk categories directly to Google’s Gemini through Firebase AI Logic to create a short comment. It sends only the combination of walk topics and, depending on those topics, how you moved (walking or cart), a category for how long you paused, weather category, daylight, time of day, built-in marker types (pee, poop, favorite, or caution), a wording number, and display language (Japanese or English). These are categories only; no numbers such as distance, time, or counts are sent. Marker types and marker-derived topic categories are sent only for walks that end after you consent to this version. It does not send coordinates, routes, photos, videos, your dog’s name, free-form text such as notes, walk IDs, or exact start/end times. If you do not consent, the walk is under 20 minutes, generation fails, or the response is empty, invalid, or contains a prohibited expression, the app selects from varied on-device templates based on the walk. Gemini and template comments are saved once and are not generated or selected again for the same walk. The app checks Gemini text for prohibited expressions on the device, but cannot guarantee that every unintended expression is prevented. You can change this setting anytime in the app.

03 · How we use location

How we use location

Location is used only for the features above. We do not use it for ads, marketing, or to build a list of who walked where.

If you choose Always Allow, the app can keep recording the route while the phone is in your pocket. You can still start a walk with While Using the App.

04 · Account and groups

Account and groups

You can record walks and keep profiles of your own dogs without an account. You sign in with Apple only to create a group, join a group, or buy Plus. The information in this section is stored on our server only when you sign in and use groups or Plus.

Sign-in

We use Sign in with Apple with Firebase Authentication. Firebase Authentication stores the user identifier issued by Apple and the email address Apple provides (an Apple relay address if you choose Hide My Email). Wampo does not use this email address for any feature and does not contact you with it.

What you share in a group

What you put in a group is visible to that group's members. It is not shown to anyone outside the group.

  • Your name: the name shown in the group, in the member list and on your posts and replies.
  • Group: the group name, members, invitations, and which groups you are in.
  • Dog profiles: for a dog you add to a group, its name, breed, birthday, photo, the setting for whether its round icon shows the photo or an avatar (with the avatar's parts, if one was made in an earlier version), and its goal. The photo is re-encoded as a JPEG of up to 1024 px on its long side and is sent without capture location or other metadata. Walk safety settings, such as a short nose or sensitivity to heat or cold, are not sent. Only the dog's owner, who is the group's admin, can add a dog to a group. The dog stays the owner's dog, and other members can only view its profile and goal.
  • Walk posts: a walk with a dog you added to a group is sent to that group when you tap Done on the walk's finish screen. You do not choose whether to send each walk. This includes the dog, start and end times, distance, duration, markers (type and location), route, and the photos and videos you choose on that screen with their capture locations. Past walks that have not been sent are sent only when you tap Send under "Unsent walks" in the group.
  • Updates and replies: the text and time of an update, the photos and videos you choose with their capture locations, and the text of a reply. An update is sent to the group when you save it.
  • The owner's dog's records: walks, updates, and replies that members send about the owner's dog are kept on our server with the owner, as the owner's dog's records, with their route, markers, photos, and videos. They stay on the server even after the group ends, but other members can no longer see them. If the owner adds the dog to another group, that group's members can also see the dog's earlier records. They are also saved on the owner's device, with who recorded them, for what arrives while the group continues. They stay on the device as the owner's dog's records, even after the group ends. After the person who recorded them leaves or is removed from the group, their name is hidden and shown as "No name". If the person who recorded them deletes their account, the owner's device deletes their records the next time it opens the app signed in.

Reporting and blocking

You can report another member's post or reply to the Operator with Report in its menu. A report goes the same way as In-app contact and sends whether it is a post or a reply, plus the identifiers needed to find it (the group, the post, the reply, and the account identifier of the person who wrote it). It does not include the text, photos or videos, or the account identifier of the person who reported it. The Operator uses the identifiers to review the post and handles it under the Terms of Use. The person who wrote it is not told that you reported it.

The list of people you block (their account identifiers and the names shown for them) is stored on that device. To stop notifications about their posts and replies, only the account identifier of the person you block is saved with your own notification settings on the server, and it is removed when you unblock them. The names shown for them are not sent to the server. They are not told that you blocked them.

You can keep locations out with Privacy Mode in Settings. Posts sent while it is on include no route, marker locations, or photo and video capture locations (marker types, distance, and duration are still sent). While it is off, these are sent as they are. The app does not automatically hide the area around the start point or your home. Changing the setting does not change posts already sent. The new setting applies to posts sent after the change. Capture locations are never written into the photo or video files and are sent as separate information. When you attach photos from the share sheet while Privacy Mode is on, their capture locations are also removed (photos whose locations cannot be removed are not attached). Whether an image made from the share sheet shows the roads and place names of the map is chosen separately from Privacy Mode, each time you share, with “Hide map” on the share sheet.

To show photos, videos, and routes, we issue short-lived links each time they are viewed, and we count the amount downloaded for the monthly per-person limit. Files left unfinished during upload expire and are removed after 24 hours.

Buying Plus

You buy Plus on the App Store. We check the purchase with RevenueCat, which receives your Wampo user identifier, used to match the purchase, and the App Store purchase record. Our server stores your Plus status, its expiry, when it was checked, and a record linking the group to the purchase. Neither Wampo nor RevenueCat receives your payment details, such as card numbers.

06 · Storage and retention

Storage and retention

Walk records (routes, distance, duration, markers, dog profiles, photos) are stored in a database on the device. What you send to a group is stored on our server (Firebase), separately from the records on your device. Deleting a group post or leaving a group does not delete the records on your device.

We keep what is stored on our server until:

  • Posts, updates, and replies: you delete them, the owner deletes the dog, or you or the owner deletes their account. Even after the group ends, they stay with the owner as the owner's dog's records. Once deleted, no one in the group can see them. Records of the owner's dog that already reached the owner's device stay on that device. If you leave a group, your posts and replies stay in it. The names of people who left are hidden and shown as "No name". A post you withdraw while you are in the group stays on the owner's device with your name. You can still delete them from Settings → Groups after you leave or after the group ends.
  • A group whose admin's Plus has ended: members can view and reply for 90 days from the day we confirm Plus has ended. After that, the group can no longer be viewed and its data is deleted. Posts, updates, and replies stay with the owner as the owner's dog's records.
  • A disbanded group: all members lose access at once, and the group's data is deleted. Posts, updates, and replies stay with the owner as the owner's dog's records. There is no 90-day period.
  • Notification device registrations: while you receive notifications. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Your account: until you delete it. What is deleted is described in Deleting data and withdrawing consent.

Data of a group that can no longer be viewed (its name, members, invites, and notifications) is deleted, as a rule, within 7 days. This does not include the dogs' records that stay with the owner. Backups and copies inside services such as Firebase are removed when each service's retention period ends. Deletion in Wampo is complete without waiting for them.

A backup you export from the app's settings contains the records on your device. It does not include the text or photos of group posts and replies, except for the copies below. Copies saved on your device of walks and moments that other members sent about your dogs are included with their text, photos, videos, comments, and the members' names and account identifiers. However, the names of people who left the group or deleted their account are not included. It also includes the last fetched name of each dog you added to a group, and a note of each post you sent (such as the group's name), with the identifiers that link them to your groups (your account and group identifiers). Group records load again when you sign in. Do not give your backup file to others.

07 · Sharing

Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not give it to anyone except as described above. What you put in a group is shown only to that group's members. What leaves the device is the minimum location needed for features, the information for groups and your account described above, usage analytics after you agree, and the walk categories listed above in older feature-bearing apps when you separately consent to Gemini comments for walks of 20 minutes or more. Version 1.16.0 (213) and later does not send data to Gemini. If you use voice input, audio may go to the OS speech recognizer (Apple or Google). We do not send audio to our own servers.

What you put in a group is given to that group's members when you choose to send it there. Handling needed for features, such as storage and delivery on our server, is entrusted to the service providers listed above.

Third parties in other countries

These providers are in the United States and handle the information described above in the United States and other countries.

  • Google LLC (Firebase, Google Maps)
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (checking Plus purchases)
  • Apple Inc. (sign-in, purchases, notifications)
  • Resend (delivering in-app contact email)

The United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law equivalent to Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information; it has sector-specific federal laws and state laws. For details, see the Personal Information Protection Commission of Japan's overview of the United States (in Japanese).

These providers maintain systems for handling personal data appropriately through their data processing terms (Google, RevenueCat, Resend) or an international certification (Apple's Global CBPR). We check regularly that these systems remain in place. On request, we will promptly tell you about these systems and the measures we take. Please use Contact.

08 · Deleting data and withdrawing consent

Deleting data and withdrawing consent

  • Records on the device: deleting the app deletes the walk records and profiles stored on the device.
  • Group posts: use Delete post on a walk's details to delete each post. Its photos, videos, routes, and replies are also removed from our server. Records of the owner's dog that already reached the owner's device stay on that device. You can still delete them from Settings → Groups after you leave or after the group ends. Screenshots and other copies saved by others cannot be removed.
  • Deleting a dog: when the owner deletes a dog, we delete the dog's photo and the posts, updates, and replies of that dog alone (with their photos, videos, and routes) from our server. The dog is removed from posts shared with other dogs.
  • Usage analytics: turn it off in Settings to withdraw consent. After that, we do not send new data to Firebase Analytics. How long Google keeps older data follows Google's policy.
  • Only in feature-bearing versions 1.16.0 (212) and earlier — Walk comments: turn this off in Settings to stop sending later walk categories to Gemini. The app will show an on-device template, as it does for walks under 20 minutes.
  • Location, camera, photos, notifications, microphone, and speech recognition: you can turn off access for this app in iOS Settings at any time.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. We accept the request after you sign in again to confirm it is you. Once accepted, deletion continues even if you close the app. We aim to finish within 30 days of acceptance. If it cannot proceed automatically, the app tells you to contact us, and we finish it once you do.

  • What is deleted: your posts, updates, and replies in every group, with their photos, videos, and routes; your name; the record of groups you were in; notification device registrations; the record of your Plus status; your sign-in information (Firebase Authentication); and your RevenueCat customer record. We also ask Apple to revoke Sign in with Apple. If we cannot confirm this, the app shows you how to revoke it yourself.
  • Only the dog's owner, who is the group's admin, can add a dog to a group. When the owner deletes their account, we delete the dog's profile, goals, and photo, and the walks, updates, and replies kept as the dog's records (including what other members sent) from our server. On other members' devices, only the copies the owner wrote are deleted.
  • Copies on the owner's device: copies of walks, updates, and replies you sent about the owner's dog that already reached the owner's device are deleted the next time that device opens the app signed in. Copies restored from a backup are deleted the same way.
  • If you are the admin of a group, that group is disbanded first.
  • Your App Store subscription is not canceled automatically. Cancel it in the App Store.
  • Walk records and photos on this device are not deleted. To delete them, delete the app or remove them separately.
09 · Security measures

Security measures

To protect the personal data we handle on our server, we take these measures.

  • Basic policy: we follow applicable laws and guidelines, and we take questions and complaints through Contact.
  • Handling rules: for each stage, from collection, use, storage, and sharing to deletion, we set how data is handled and how long it is kept (see Storage and retention).
  • Organization: Wampo is run by one individual. Only the developer handles personal data.
  • People: the developer keeps checking what to watch for when handling personal data.
  • Physical: devices used for administration are protected with a screen lock against loss or theft.
  • Technical: access to data on our server is limited to you and the members of your groups. Connections are encrypted, and photos, videos, and routes are delivered through short-lived links. App Check blocks requests that do not come from the genuine app.
  • Understanding the foreign environment: we take these measures after understanding the privacy laws of the United States, where personal data is handled (see Sharing).

Support →

In-app contact

When you choose to send, the category, subject, message and optional reply email go through Google Cloud Functions and Resend to a fixed support inbox. App version, OS version and device type (OS) are sent only when individually selected. The reply email is contact information in the message body; a reply is not guaranteed.

Walk history, location, photos, videos, dog names, backups, app logs, advertising IDs, user IDs and persistent device IDs are not attached. Avoid including these in your message. A report sends the identifiers described in Reporting and blocking as the message. Drafts remain only in app memory, not in persistent device storage.

The subject, message and reply email are not stored in Firestore or application logs. Firestore holds only keyed digests for duplicate prevention, status, timestamps and aggregate sending counters. Duplicate-prevention metadata becomes logically invalid after 25 hours; asynchronous TTL deletion occurs later and is not a 25-hour deletion guarantee. Google and Resend may record network metadata such as IP addresses in service operation logs.

Delivered messages and reply emails are handled by Resend and the support inbox. Provider retention and support retention/deletion practices must be confirmed and disclosed before in-app sending is enabled. For deletion requests, use the existing support contact on this page. Web support and the public email remain available when in-app sending is unavailable.
