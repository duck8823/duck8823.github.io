04 · Groups

Groups

A group lets the people you invite share your dogs' walks and moments with each other.

  • Admin: the person who created the group. Creating a group requires Plus. Only the admin, as the dogs' owner, can add dogs to the group.
  • Members: people who joined by accepting the admin's invitation. A group can have up to 6 people, including the admin. One person can be in up to 3 groups.
  • Leaving: members can leave a group at any time. Their posts and replies stay in the group, shown without their name.
  • Disbanding: the admin can disband the group at any time. Once disbanded, no member can see it, and its data is deleted. A disbanded group cannot be restored.
  • When the admin's Plus ends: for 90 days from the day we confirm it ended, members can still view and reply. If the admin restarts Plus during that time, posting resumes. After 90 days, the group can no longer be viewed and its data is deleted.
  • Records on your device: records saved on each device remain even after the group is gone.
  • Blocking: you can block another member from the member list on the group's page (tapping the person who wrote a post or reply takes you to that group's page). Once blocked, that device stops showing their group posts and replies, and you stop getting notifications about their posts and replies. Walks and moments that already reached your device as records of your own dog stay. They are not notified. You can unblock them in the same places or from Blocked People on the Groups screen.
