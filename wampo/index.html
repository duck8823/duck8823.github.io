Make Every Dog Walk
Memorable

Wampo is a simple walking tracker app that records your dog walks with GPS. Review routes, distances, and times. Place markers at interesting spots and get walking time suggestions based on weather.

Features

Everything you need for a great walking experience.

🗺️

Route Recording

Automatically record walking routes with GPS. View distance and time in real time.

📍

Markers

Place markers at interesting spots during walks. Record restroom stops, favorite places, or caution areas on the map.

🌤️

Weather Integration

Get walking time suggestions based on precipitation, temperature, and wind speed from meteorological data.

🐕

Dog Profiles

Register multiple dogs and manage walk records for each one.

📊

Walk History

Review past walks in a list. Check routes, distances, and times in detail.

🔒

Privacy First

All data stays on your device. No external servers, no account registration required.
