06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Reach out from Support.

Is it really free?
Yes. Calorithm is free to download and use, supported by in-app advertising. Optional Calorithm Plus is available on iOS in Japan. Android remains Free-only. Recording and basic summaries are available for free.
How does the AI estimate work?
Enter a food name to get an estimated calorie value from AI. You can adjust the value before saving. AI usage limits apply in the app. The form on this page is an interaction example, not real AI.
Is my data kept private?
Health data and meal records are primarily stored on your device. When you use AI features, the input, photos, or record summaries needed for processing may be sent to external AI services. See the privacy policy for providers and data handling.
Is there an Android version?
Yes. The Android version is now available on Google Play.
How do I transfer my records and settings?
Use the app's backup feature to save an encrypted file and restore your records and settings. Restoring a purchase does not restore those records. Keep your backup file and passphrase before reinstalling or changing devices. See Support for the steps.
Apple Watch or Wear OS?
Not at the moment. Wearable support is being considered for future updates.
