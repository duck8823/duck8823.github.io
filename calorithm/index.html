Daily Balance

Auto-syncs with Apple Health to display your calorie intake and expenditure side by side. See your energy balance at a glance — no manual tracking of activity needed.

Timeline

See all your meals, exercises, and weight entries in one chronological feed. Quickly review what you logged and when, making it easy to spot patterns in your daily routine.

AI Calorie Estimation

Just type a food name and let AI estimate the calories for you. AI also estimates active energy for exercises. No need to search through databases — get quick, intelligent estimates to keep your logging fast and simple.

Weekly Trends & Weight

View your calorie balance trends and weight changes over the week with intuitive charts. Spot patterns, stay motivated, and make informed decisions about your diet.

Preset Favorites

Save your frequently eaten meals as presets and log them with a single tap. No more re-entering the same foods every day — fast, convenient calorie logging.

Widget & Notifications

Add a widget to your home screen to see your calorie balance at a glance. Enable push notifications to receive your daily calorie balance summary.
