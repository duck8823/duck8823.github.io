About Calorithm

Calorithm focuses on simplicity and effectiveness, helping you understand your energy balance at a glance. By connecting with Health, the app automatically retrieves your activity data and combines it with your manually entered food intake to give you a complete picture of your calorie balance.

Whether you're trying to maintain, lose, or gain weight, Calorithm provides the insights you need to make informed decisions about your diet and exercise routine.
