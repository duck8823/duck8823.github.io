02

The service

The App shows your calorie balance, trends, and reports based on the meals, exercise, and weight you record and the values read from Apple Health or Health Connect. Recording and basic summaries are available for free. While you are on Free, ads are shown in the App.

Plus is an optional paid plan. It adds comparisons and analyses, an expanded AI allowance beyond Free, no ads, and more. The features included in Plus are shown on the purchase screen in the App.

Features of the App may be added, changed, or discontinued for improvement or operational reasons.

04

Free trial

Eligible users can try Plus free for 14 days. The store determines eligibility, and it is shown on the purchase screen. Users who have already had a trial, for example, are not eligible.

To avoid being charged when the trial ends, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you purchased on the App Store, or before the trial ends if you purchased on Google Play. If you do not cancel, the price of the plan you chose is charged when the trial ends, and the paid subscription begins. The date the trial ends is shown on the purchase screen.

05

Automatic renewal

Unless you cancel, your Plus subscription renews automatically at each billing interval, and you are charged each time it renews.

If you switch from monthly to yearly, the yearly plan starts from the next renewal date.

If payment fails, you may lose access to Plus after the period set by the store.

06

How to cancel

Cancel from the subscription management of the store where you purchased.

  • Purchased on the App Store: open your Apple Account in the iPhone Settings app and cancel from Subscriptions.
  • Purchased on Google Play: cancel from Subscriptions in Google Play.
Deleting the App does not cancel your subscription.

After you cancel, you can keep using Plus until the end of the period you have already paid for. Your records stay as they are after you cancel.

07

Refunds

Because Plus is paid for through the store, please request a refund from the store where you purchased. Whether a refund is granted is decided under each store's rules.

  • Purchased on the App Store: request a refund from Apple
  • Purchased on Google Play: request a refund from Google Play

If you have a problem, such as being unable to use Plus because of a bug, please contact us. We will check the details and tell you what we can do.

When we confirm that the subscription is no longer valid because of a refund or revocation, Plus is no longer available.

08

Store and supported OS

Plus can be used only on the OS of the store where you purchased it. Plus purchased on the App Store works only on iOS, and Plus purchased on Google Play works only on Android. Plus cannot be moved to or shared with the OS of another store.

On a new device or after reinstalling the App, use Restore Purchases with the same store account you purchased with. Restoring a purchase restores only your Plus subscription, not your meal, weight, or other records. Use the App's backup to transfer your records.

09

AI features

The App's AI features (estimating meals and activity, consultations, and more) have usage limits. Plus provides an expanded AI allowance beyond Free. Check the App for how much you can use and how much each use takes.

In the App Store version, you may be able to buy additional AI points. The conditions are shown on the purchase screen in the App. They are not sold in the Google Play version.

When you use AI features, the App asks for your consent before sending, and then sends the necessary input or summaries of your records to external AI services. See the Privacy Policy for details.

AI answers and estimates are guides only. We do not guarantee that they are accurate or fit for your purpose. Please check the content and values before saving.
