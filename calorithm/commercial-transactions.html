01

Seller

The seller's statutory legal name, address, and phone number are disclosed electronically without delay upon request, with enough time before a purchase decision. This repository and this page do not store those values.

Request intake and response are operated off-repository by the owner. A green page check does not prove legal compliance. Use the contact page for general inquiries.

02

Prices

Monthly JPY 280 including tax, billing period P1M. Yearly JPY 2,400 including tax, billing period P1Y. Currency is Japanese yen.

Eligible new subscribers receive a 14-day free period. Whether a trial is shown follows StoreKit eligibility and the introductory offer.

03

Payment, service start, and renewal

Payment is charged to the Apple Account through the App Store. Plus service starts after the purchase is verified.

Subscriptions renew automatically. Network charges are the customer's responsibility.

04

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel from Apple subscription management. After cancellation, Plus remains available until the current period ends. Refunds follow Apple's refund process.

05

Availability and related documents

Plus is sold for iOS in Japan. Android remains Free-only in this release.

See the privacy policy and contact pages on this site. Terms of Use is the Apple Standard EULA at https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/. The Japanese label 利用規約 and the English label Terms of Use target that same URI.

This page has no purchase button.
