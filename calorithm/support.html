01

Getting started

On iOS, Calorithm uses Apple Health; on Android, it uses Health Connect. Follow the app prompts to allow reading and writing the data you want to use. Steps and sleep are read only with permission. You can change permissions in your device’s Health / Health Connect settings. Data cannot be shown when permission is missing or no source record exists.

No. Available activity data varies by device and connected service. If you use a fitness device, first check that its records have reached Apple Health / Health Connect.

Recording food, exercise and weight, viewing your balance, standard trends and reports, and manual encrypted backup and import are available for free. AI is available with consent within your points allowance. Android is Free-only.

02

Records and reports

Yes. Enter values in the recording screen and save them. You can also select foods to log. Manual recording remains available without AI consent or when you run out of points.

Open the record in the timeline and review its contents before editing. Check the selected record before deleting it. The balance uses the records for the selected date, so also check the date and time.

No. Standard reports summarize records on your device without AI consent, a network connection or points. AI insights and consultations are separate features. Consultations need explicit sending and consent and use points. Neither reports nor AI text provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

03

AI and points

AI estimates food from names or photos, and activity from details such as exercise names and duration. Estimates are approximate and vary with portions, ingredients and preparation. Check the result before saving and adjust it using food labels and actual quantities. AI answers and health outcomes are not guaranteed.

Before sending to an external AI service, the app explains the recipient, shared items and purpose and asks for consent. Without consent, it does not send. Google processes estimation and related features; OpenAI processes consultations. You can withdraw consent in settings. See the Privacy Policy for details of what is shared.

Free points are granted per calendar month in Japan Standard Time. Paid Plus points are granted monthly from the subscription date, including yearly plans; months without that day use the last day. Granted points do not carry into the next grant period. Check your balance and the points needed for a request in the app. Web search uses more points than a normal estimate or consultation.

With no usable points, AI estimates and consultations are unavailable until the next grant. Manual recording and standard reports remain available. If Plus ends and you return to Free in the same Japan Standard Time month, the remaining Free points from before Plus are still available.

On iOS, you can buy extra points when purchasing is available in the app. Check the app’s purchase screen for details and conditions. Purchased points do not expire. Granted points are used first, then purchased points. Purchased points remain usable on Free after Plus ends and remain when you withdraw AI consent. Purchases are not available on Android.

Web search is available only with Plus. Text-based food estimation uses Google Search and consultations use OpenAI web search when needed. Search uses more points than a normal request. Toggle AI Search in settings. When too few points remain, estimation and consultation proceed without search.

Up to the latest 30 conversations are kept on your device. Free can view the latest 3, and Plus the latest 30. Deleted conversations do not return when you subscribe again.

04

Plus and restoring purchases

Plus is an optional paid plan for iOS in Japan. Check the app’s purchase screen and App Store for prices and applicable conditions. Android is Free-only, with no Plus or points purchases.

Free recording features remain available. Plus expands the AI points allowance, web search and consultation history access, adds comparison lines, extra trend cards, expenditure estimation and additional report analysis, and removes ads.

Open subscriptions under your Apple Account in iPhone Settings and cancel through Apple’s subscription management. Subscriptions renew automatically, so check renewal and cancellation terms on the purchase screen. Plus remains available until the current period ends, and your records remain.

No. Restore Purchases in settings restores the Plus subscription purchased with the same Apple Account. It does not restore on-device records such as food entries or conversations. Before changing devices or reinstalling, keep an encrypted backup and its passphrase.

05

Understanding your balance

The basic calculation is intake − expenditure. Intake is the energy from logged food. Expenditure uses available measured or estimated values such as active and resting energy. Positive means intake exceeds expenditure; negative means expenditure exceeds intake. The sign alone does not mean good or bad.

No. Missing food entries do not mean you ate nothing, and unavailable activity data does not mean zero activity. Screens and summaries differ in how they show and count missing data. Do not interpret totals or averages with missing days as if every day had complete records.

They are estimates based on food records, Health measurements and any estimation you use. Missing entries, permissions and device or service synchronization can change them. They do not guarantee changes to your body or medical outcomes.

06

Troubleshooting and backup

Check the selected date, whether source records exist in Apple Health / Health Connect, and whether the necessary permissions are allowed. Syncing from a device or service can take time. Reopen Calorithm after syncing and contact support if the problem remains. Do not treat unavailable data as zero.

Try reopening the app, restarting the device and checking for an available app update. If the problem remains, use the contact page to send your device, OS and app versions and steps to reproduce. Do not delete or reinstall the app without checking your backup, because records may be lost.

Manually export an encrypted backup from settings and safely keep both the file and the passphrase used to create it. This is not automatic backup. There is no recovery key if you forget the passphrase. Apple Health / Health Connect source data is not included; check migration and syncing with those services separately.

Check the file and the passphrase used when creating it. A file saved to the cloud may not be readable until it finishes syncing to your device. Wait and select the file again. Review the preview during import. Import does not restore Health source data or write it back to Health. Contact support if the problem remains; you do not need to send your passphrase or backup file itself.

07

Privacy and contact

Health data and food entries are primarily kept on your device. With consent, AI processes selected input and necessary summaries externally. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Use the in-app contact feature or this site’s contact page. Your device, OS and app versions, the affected screen and steps to reproduce help us investigate. Do not send passphrases or unnecessary health information.
