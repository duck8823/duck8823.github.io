Privacy Policy

Calorithm is an independently developed personal project. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Calorithm mobile application (the “App”).

Last updatedSeptember 30, 2026
Reading5 min read

Local

Stays on your device

Health data and food entries are primarily stored locally on your device. AI features send the input and record summaries needed for processing to external services. Google processes estimation, suggestions, and reports; OpenAI processes consultations. Photos are sent to Google for estimation. The app explains what is shared and with whom before requesting consent.

Encrypted

AES-256 protected

Sensitive data — including body measurements, AI estimation history, and generated AI reports — is stored in an encrypted on-device database.

Never sold

Not sold

Your personal data is not sold or traded. Third-party services may process data as needed for AI processing, analytics, crash diagnostics, and advertising delivery.

Information collected

The App handles the following types of information.

Health data

Calorithm integrates with Apple Health and Google Health Connect to access and display the following health-related data.

TypeOperation
Active EnergyRead
Resting EnergyRead
WeightRead & Write
Dietary Energy ConsumedRead & Write
Body Fat PercentageRead & Write
StepsRead
SleepRead

This data is used to provide insights about calorie balance, body composition, and weight trends. Raw data retrieved from Apple Health / Google Health Connect is processed locally on your device; when you use AI features, only the necessary summaries may be used for AI processing.

Steps and sleep are read only if you allow it; Calorithm never writes them. They are used to show today’s steps and last night’s sleep on the dashboard, and to show day-to-day changes in trend charts and Reflection reports. When you use AI features, the only step and sleep data that may be sent is summarized values: daily and period step totals, and each night’s sleep time, time in bed, bedtime, and nap time, plus their averages. Individual sleep records are not sent.

On Android, after you grant access, Calorithm may also read Health Connect data in the background — including while the app is not on screen — to keep calorie balance and body composition up to date. Reads are not limited to times when the app is in the foreground.

If health data carries over via iCloud, that is Apple Health’s own sync. Calorithm does not store health information in CloudKit.

Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.
Calorithm is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Reflections and AI-generated text provide information inferred from your records. They are not medical, diagnostic, or treatment advice, or individualized nutritional guidance. Consult a healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

User-provided information

You can manually enter calorie intake. This information is stored locally on your device. When you use AI features, your input, photos, or summaries derived from your records may be transmitted for AI processing.

Body measurements you provide (such as height, gender, age) are stored in an encrypted local database and are used solely for calorie balance estimation.

Camera & photo library

When you choose to estimate calories from a food photo, Calorithm may access your device's camera or photo library. Photos are sent to Google AI services (Firebase AI Logic / Vertex AI Gemini) solely for AI processing. The App itself does not store photos externally.

Purchase information (iOS)

When you subscribe to Plus or buy additional AI points on iOS, the App's server verifies the App Store's signed transaction information and uses it to grant points and manage your balance. The server stores keyed hashes of the purchase and subscription identifiers, together with the state of point grants, balances, and refunds. The App does not receive your payment method or name. Android has no purchase features.

In-app inquiries

When you contact us from within the App, the App sends the category you chose, your message, your reply-to email address, and diagnostic information (app and OS version, device model, Plus status, error type and time, and the screen the contact form was opened from). A reply-to address is required for the How to use, Purchases and billing, and Privacy and data categories, and optional for the others. You can choose not to send diagnostic information. This information reaches the developer's email through Resend, an email delivery service, and is used only to respond to your inquiry. The App's server does not store your message or reply-to address.

Usage data

In-app usage analytics and crash reporting are disabled by default. They are collected only after you separately opt in to each category in Settings, and you can withdraw either consent at any time. The website may collect minimal usage information such as page views.

  • App feature usage statistics
  • App performance data
  • Crash reports
  • Website page views and button clicks

The App does not send calories, meals, weight, body fat, activity, goals, or ranges or classifications derived from them to Analytics or Crashlytics. Crash reports do not include health or food data or user-entered content.

Where ad consent is required, the App presents Google's User Messaging Platform consent form and uses the resulting eligibility to decide whether ads may be requested. When required, you can review or change your choices from Ad privacy options in the App. When ads are displayed, Google Mobile Ads may collect approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, ad or in-app interactions, performance data, and other diagnostics. These may be used for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and app functionality. Health and food data are not used for advertising.

  • Develop new features and services
  • Understand how users interact with the App

Sharing your information

Your personal information is not sold or traded. Third-party services may process data as needed for AI processing, analytics, diagnostics, and advertising delivery. Health data and meal records are not used for advertising.

Each service processes data under its own privacy policy, contract, and terms. Google processes estimation, suggestions, and reports, and photos are sent to Google for estimation. AI features may process the selected input and necessary summaries of meals, activity, steps, sleep, weight, body fat, goals, preferences, health considerations, allergies, and optional notes. The content of AI consultations (your message, the earlier conversation, and the record summaries the consultation needs) is sent to OpenAI and may be processed in the United States or other countries. Before the first transfer to Google's AI service or to OpenAI, the App displays the recipient, the data categories, and the purpose, and obtains explicit consent. If you decline, no data is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time in the App's Settings. OpenAI states that data sent to its API is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in.

Consultations may include your message, part of the recent conversation, goals and preferences, summaries of today’s calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, activity and steps, and the profile you entered. When you consult about a reflection, the report you confirmed for sending may also be included, including summarized sleep information and additional analysis shown in Plus. Raw sleep records are not sent. When you reopen a conversation and send another message, its saved report summary may be attached again.

Google Search for meal estimation (Plus)

When search is enabled with Plus, text-based meal estimation may use Google Search when official nutrition information for a chain restaurant, brand, or product is needed. The AI is instructed not to search for generic dishes or ingredients. Search terms are generated from your input.

For this Google Search feature, Google may retain search terms and related context for up to 3 days for debugging the search infrastructure, without associating it with you. This describes search-related processing, not the retention period for all AI data.

Estimates that used search show the titles of the pages they referred to, and Google Search Suggestions right after the result is received. Tapping a page or a suggestion opens it in your external browser, where that site's terms apply (Google's Terms of Service for Google pages). The App does not record which pages you open.

  • Google Service Specific Terms (Grounding with Google Search)
  • OpenAI API data controls

Web search in consultations (Plus)

In Plus consultations, the AI may use OpenAI web search to check publicly available information when needed. Consultations on the free plan do not use search. The search terms are generated by the AI from your question. The AI is instructed not to include personal information, such as your meal or weight records or your health considerations, in search terms.

Search terms are sent to OpenAI and its search providers to perform the search, and are handled under their respective data retention policies.

Answers that used search show the title and domain of the pages they referred to. Tapping one opens that page in your external browser, where that site's terms apply. The App does not record which pages you open.

05

Data security

Sensitive personal data — including body measurements, AI estimation history, and generated AI reports — is stored in an AES-256 encrypted database on your device. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

06

Your data rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data.

  • Usage analytics: disabled by default; enable or withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy & Data > Usage Analytics.
  • Ad choices: make your selection in Google's UMP consent form. Where required, review or change it in Settings > Privacy & Data > Ad privacy options. On iOS, ATT separately controls cross-app tracking.
  • Crash reporting: disabled by default and controlled independently from usage analytics.
  • AI data sharing: withdrawing consent in Settings stops new AI transfers and deletes the AI responses temporarily stored on the server and the record of your AI allowance. The balance of purchased points remains. Requests already processed cannot be undone.
  • Consultation history: you can delete it from this device. Deleting a conversation does not delete logged meals or the original reflection, and does not mean that data already processed by external services is immediately erased.
  • Data deletion questions: contact us at the email below.
