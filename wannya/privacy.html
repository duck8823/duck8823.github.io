Privacy Policy

Our Commitment

wannya ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Storage

All data entered in the App (pet profiles, medication records, health records, weight records, daily logs, etc.) is stored exclusively on your device.

  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • There is no automatic cloud synchronization
  • No data is shared with third parties

Device Permissions

Notifications

Used to send reminders for medication schedules and health management. All notification processing is completed on the device; no external servers are involved.

Camera & Photo Library

Used to take or select photos for pet profiles and health records. Photos are stored only on the device and are never uploaded externally.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, advertising, or tracking.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App from your device permanently deletes all stored data. You can also delete individual data at any time from within the App's settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not targeted at any specific age group and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
