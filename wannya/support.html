Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. All data is stored exclusively on your device. No data is sent to external servers or synchronized with cloud services.

Q. What happens to my data when I switch devices?

Currently, full data migration (backup & restore) is not available. You can export health records as CSV / PDF, but transferring all data to a new device is not yet supported. We are considering this feature for future updates.

Q. How many pets can I register?

You can register up to 3 pets for free.

Q. What can I record in daily logs?

You can record daily activities such as meals, exercise, bathroom habits, sleep, playtime, and more.

Q. I'm not receiving medication schedule notifications

Please check the following:

  1. Go to device Settings > Notifications and ensure wannya notifications are enabled
  2. Check that notifications are enabled in the medication schedule settings within the app
  3. Ensure Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is not active

Q. The weight chart is not showing any data

The weight chart requires at least two weight records to display. Please record weight data from the Health Records section first.

Troubleshooting

If the app is not working properly

  1. Force quit the app and relaunch it
  2. Restart your device
  3. Update to the latest version from the App Store

If notifications are not arriving

  1. Go to device Settings > Notifications > wannya and enable notifications
  2. Ensure Banners, Sounds, and Badges are all enabled
  3. Check that Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is turned off
